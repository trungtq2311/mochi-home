## Long description

Meet Mochi, a cheerful little cat who just inherited a tired old house. Solve colourful match-3 puzzles, earn stars and spend them to repair, furnish and decorate every room — your choice of style each time!

SWIPE, MATCH, BLAST
• Match 3 or more pieces to clear the board and complete level goals.
• Make rockets, bombs, paper planes and rainbow balls — and combine them for huge combos.
• Break wooden boxes and clean up dirt to bring every room back to life.
• Hundreds of hand-tuned levels that stay relaxing: friendly difficulty, helpful hints and free boosters when you get stuck.

DECORATE YOUR WAY
• Restore the living room, kitchen, garden and more.
• Pick between several styles for every sofa, lamp and flowerbed.
• Watch Mochi react to every change you make.

ALWAYS SOMETHING TO DO
• Daily gift calendar, daily quests and 3-day festivals.
• Race other cats in the daily race for extra rewards.

PLAY ANYWHERE
• Works fully offline — perfect for the subway or a flight.
• No account, no ads, no data collection. Your progress is saved on your device.
• English and Vietnamese.

Cozy up with Mochi and make a house a home!
